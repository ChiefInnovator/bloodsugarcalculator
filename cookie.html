Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are and how we use them on the eAG/A1C Diabetes Calculator website. You should read this policy so you can understand what type of cookies we use, the information we collect using cookies and how that information is used.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They allow the website to recognize your device and remember some information about your preferences or past actions.

Storage used by this calculator

Your preferences are saved in this browser’s localStorage under cookieConsent for up to 12 months. This is device storage, not a cookie. It holds your analytics/advertising choices, a version, and the time you saved them. The calculator does not save health measurements or unit preferences there.

No analytics or advertising tracking loader is currently configured in this application. The controls save a consent signal; selecting Accept All does not add a tracker. Rejecting optional consent does not prevent calculations.

External requests and sharing

The Tailwind stylesheet and Google Fonts are requested from external providers regardless of these preferences. Those requests disclose network information such as your IP address. Links to donation, social, email and ChatGPT destinations have their own privacy policies.

New calculation links place values and a timestamp in a URL fragment. Anyone receiving a link can decode it. Legacy query links send their encoded values to hosting on the initial request before this page migrates the current address. See the privacy policy for data-transfer details.

Your Choices Regarding Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie preferences by clicking the "Manage Cookie Preferences" button below or in the footer of any page. Your preferences will be remembered for 12 months.

You can also control cookies through your web browser settings. Most web browsers allow some control of most cookies through the browser settings. To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set, visit www.aboutcookies.org or www.allaboutcookies.org.

Consent Duration

Your cookie consent preferences are stored for 12 months. After this period, you will be asked to confirm your preferences again.

Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time in order to reflect, for example, changes to the cookies we use or for other operational, legal or regulatory reasons. Please therefore re-visit this Cookie Policy regularly to stay informed about our use of cookies and related technologies.
